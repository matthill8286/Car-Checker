## What the app does as a result

- Every answer from a source is stored verbatim in `source_responses.raw`, alongside what the adapter made of it.
- An answer is reused for 24 hours (DVLA, DVSA, RDW) or 30 days (NHTSA), and a "not found" for 1 hour. "Check again" in the UI, or `"fresh": true` on the API, asks every source again. See `apps/api/src/checks/cache.ts`.
- The web app's footer carries the OGL attribution and credits RDW and NHTSA.
- The registration is the only identifier stored; no keeper or personal data is requested from any source.
